MY BEGINNING

MY STORY

I started my path on tech by working on chromebook repair during my sophmore, junior and senior year at Penn Highschool and I genuinely enjoyed the physical side of technology. So when I was applying for college, I met with one of the advisors, later on I realized it was the chair of the computer science department, and I explained to him that I wanted to work on computers at the hardware level and he was like, "oh we can do that for you" and enrolled me in the computer science pathway.

Now you might be wondering, computer science is more on the software and understanding computers rather than just working on them and to that I would agree with you, however along the pathway I found interest in databases. I just found it facsinating all the intricacies that data can flow in and out of those databases and how data no matter how big or small makes our world run round and round. So while my starting point was an accident, a miscommunication some would say, I don't think I would've ever found the field that I enjoy so much without it happening.
